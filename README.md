# Banking & Loan Risk Intelligence Dashboard 🏦
Power BI dashboard analyzing a ₹50.20Bn loan portfolio (10,000+ records) — tracking default rate (13.77%), high-risk exposure, and defaulted exposure using DAX, Power Query, and interactive drill-through reporting

**** - Project developed as part of my Data Analytics role Credgenics.*

An end-to-end Power BI solution for credit risk monitoring, portfolio analysis, and borrower segmentation — built on a loan portfolio of **10,000 customers** worth **₹50.20Bn**.

## 📊 Overview
This 3-page interactive dashboard helps banking and lending teams monitor portfolio health, identify high-risk borrowers, and segment customers for targeted risk management and business decisions.

## 📁 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Portfolio & Risk Overview
High-level snapshot of the entire loan book:
- **10,000** total customers | **₹50.20Bn** total portfolio value
- **13.77%** default rate | **3,854** high-risk customers
- **₹19.54Bn** high-risk exposure | **₹7.03Bn** defaulted loan exposure
- Portfolio distribution by loan type (Home, Personal, Car, Business, Education)
- Loan status split (Active 86.23% vs Defaulted 13.77%)
- Top 10 cities by portfolio value and risk distribution (Low/Medium/High)

### 2. Credit Risk Intelligence Center
Deep-dive risk segmentation and default monitoring:
- **1,377** defaulted loans tracked across the portfolio
- Default rate by risk category, loan type, credit score band, and occupation
- High-Risk Loan Exposure Watchlist — drill-through table flagging customers with low credit scores and high default risk
- Cross-filterable by loan type, city, and risk category

### 3. Borrower Segmentation & Portfolio Insights
Customer profiling and revenue concentration analysis:
- **3,775** VIP Borrowers | **3,691** High Value Borrowers
- **₹5.02M** average revenue per borrower
- Portfolio distribution and default rate by borrower segment
- Largest individual loans table and portfolio value by city
- Key insight: VIP borrowers drive the largest share of portfolio value, while Regular Borrowers show the highest default rate

## -- Tech Stack
- **Power Query** — data cleaning, transformation, and ETL
- **Power BI Data Modeling** — relational (star-schema) data model
- **DAX** — calculated measures and KPI cards
- **Power BI Visuals** — bar, donut, and column charts with slicers, cross-filtering, and drill-through pages

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- Regular Borrowers exhibit the highest default rate despite lower loan volume
- Portfolio concentration is highest in Chennai and Bangalore
- A small VIP borrower segment contributes a disproportionate share of total revenue
- Education Loans show the highest default rate among loan types
